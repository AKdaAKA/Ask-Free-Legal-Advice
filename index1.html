To claim maintenance under section 125 of Code of Criminal Procedure Strict proof of marriage is not necessary. When the parties live together as husband and wife, there is a presumption that they are legally married couple for claim of maintenance of wife under Section 125 Cr.P.C. Unlike matrimonial proceedings where strict proof of marriage is essential, in the proceedings under Section 125 Cr.P.C., such strict standard of proof is not necessary as it is summary in nature meant to prevent vagrancy. Judgement by R.Banumathi J - Supreme Court - 24-10-2018
